Firmware Update Across Three Processors

Question

Pen and paper. Nothing is compiled, randomised or marked for you. Set the timer, answer, then compare with the solution sheet.

Allotted: 30 minutes

Update the firmware on all three processors without ever leaving the device unbootable.

The module

An edge connectivity module sits inside a home energy device, such as a battery, heat pump or EV charger. It has three processors:

ProcessorHardwareRole
Radio processorRISC-V, real-time OS. Wi-Fi 6, BLE 5.4, ThreadHTTP and MQTT links to the cloud
CoordinatorDual-core Cortex-M33, 150 MHz, 520 kB SRAM, real-time OSHardware firewall: inspects every message between device and network. Runs scheduling and user features
OEM sandboxSecond dual-core Cortex-M33, 150 MHz, 520 kB SRAMRuns the device maker's driver, written in C against an SDK. Receives simple instantaneous commands

It also has:

  • Device interfaces: GPIO, SPI at 10 MHz, I²C, PWM, UART, and 8 ADC channels at 500 kS/s combined.
  • A dedicated Modbus link to the site's grid meter.
  • Signed firmware, verified at boot. Storage encrypted with a key unique to each device.

The scenario

Assume the module receives updates over the air.

One release may change any of the three images: radio, coordinator and OEM sandbox. The OEM image is built by the device maker against the SDK.

Power can be lost at any moment. The device may be running a battery at full power when the update arrives.

Your task

  1. Draw the update flow from download to confirmed, as a state diagram.
  2. Say who verifies what, and with which key.
  3. Say how a failed update recovers, including power loss at each step.
  4. Say how you stop the coordinator and the OEM driver ending up on incompatible versions.
