Digger Drive Control

Question

Pen and paper. Nothing is compiled, randomised or marked for you. Set the timer, answer, then compare with the solution sheet.

Allotted: 20 minutes

The scenario

A digger has a drive joystick and a seat that can rotate (north / south).

The joystick is fixed to the armrest of the seat, so it turns with the operator.

Drive direction and gear selection depend on the state of these inputs.

Your task

  1. Draw the component diagram.
  2. Label each link with the data passed between the components.

Stretch prompts

Only if time remains.

  • Where does the direction logic live, and why there?
  • What happens when the seat is moving, or the sensors disagree?
  • What happens to a joystick that is held while the seat rotates?
  • What must the design do when a message is lost or corrupt?
